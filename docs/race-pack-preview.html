Your First 70.3
Race Pack Preview

Build three timing scenarios for a 1.9 km swim, 90 km bike and 21.0975 km run. Compare the individual splits, review a practical race-week checklist and print your sample plan. This preview is free; the proposed paid edition is still being developed.

01 / Build your baseline

Mobile shortcut: type 830 for an 08:30 start and 530 for a 05:30 finish target. The conservative and ambitious scenarios adjust swim pace by 5 seconds/100 m, bike speed by 1 km/h, and run pace by 10 seconds/km. These are illustrative variations, not fitness predictions.

04 / Plan your race morning

Work backwards from your start time. Add your estimated travel, parking, transition setup, warm-up and a contingency buffer. The timeline adjusts when you change the start time. Confirm all official check-in and transition zone deadlines with the organizer.

Local date & timeTask

Enter the actual race-morning closure from the official athlete guide. This is not fetched automatically; leave blank if unknown or if bike check-in is on another day.

The planner uses your device's local timezone. For a race elsewhere, confirm time-zone differences and DST against the event's official local schedule. It does not check real-time traffic or organizer access rules.

05 / Your race-week checklist

Your checklist is not uploaded. It is saved on this device only if you select Save on this device below; otherwise it is lost when this page closes.

Your race-day decisions

Write down a practical response to three situations before race day. You decide what is appropriate, using the official athlete guide and what you have tested in training. Your choices are included in the downloadable dossier and printout.

Never use this planner to override event safety instructions or medical advice. Do not include sensitive medical information in a shared export.
