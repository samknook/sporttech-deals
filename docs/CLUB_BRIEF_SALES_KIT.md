# TriZentro Club Brief — first organizer sales kit
Created 5 November 2026. Internal planning only. **No service is yet available to buy. No clients, endorsements, confirmed pilot slots or signed agreements.**

## The offer in one sentence
Turn the event-specific rules and times *supplied and approved by a triathlon club or race organizer* into a clear, bilingual participant briefing that is easy to review, distribute and print.

## Why the organizer might pay
Time saved preparing schedules, fewer copy/paste inconsistencies, a repeatable NL/EN communication template, a clean event-day PDF and a clearly documented review process. The organizer remains the source of truth and final approver. Do not claim certified race safety, legal compliance or verified official rules.

## Working demonstration
https://samknook.github.io/sporttech-deals/club-race-brief.html?lang=nl

Current demonstrator includes event details, organizer-provided URL, six key times, two waves, notes, sorted timeline, conflict warnings, text export, copy and print. The public website and full mobile browser CI still need live verification. No account, cloud sync, API or payment system.

## Proposed first paid pilot (price hypothesis, not a current offer)
**€149 per event, excluding any applicable VAT.** A manually delivered service with:
- One event's material supplied by the organizer; no collection of unapproved or sensitive participant data.
- One bilingual participant brief and print-ready PDF, prepared from the source material and reviewed with the organizer.
- Up to two feedback rounds and final organizer signoff before publication.
- A fixed scope and agreed delivery time, to be confirmed in a real proposal.

Do not quote this as a binding price until legal operator, tax treatment, contract scope and delivery workflow are in place. Avoid promise of independence or professional safety validation.

**Later**, a multi-event organizational license might be preferable if repeat use is confirmed. Annual pricing and account-based SaaS are not decided.

## Simple economics (illustrative, not a prediction)
At hypothetical €149 revenue for a 3-hour manual brief, gross revenue per working hour is €49.67 before taxes, payment fees, admin, customer support and revisions. At 5 hours the effective gross is €29.80/hour. Control scope carefully.

## Outreach controls
- No unsolicited mass email, automated scraping or claims that organizations are already clients.
- Review recipient relevance and available public organization contact channels before any personal outreach.
- Obtain the owner's approval to make external contact or send proposals.
- Do not store private correspondence or attendee details in the public GitHub repository.

## Track real evidence only
