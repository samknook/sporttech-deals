Tell us what is actually useful.

We are building digital tools and practical guides for triathletes. Use the free 70.3 Race Pack prototype or read our Blueprint sample chapters first. Then answer these six questions. We want specific criticism, not just compliments.

Try these four tasks before answering

  1. Open the free Readiness Check and mark at least three planning items.
  2. Open the Race Pack preview. Enter a realistic target, for example 530 for 05:30, and review the scenarios.
  3. Find the race-morning schedule, enter an official transition closure time only if you know it, and try the Save / Load draft buttons.
  4. Download a sample dossier, then open the Race-Day Passport and use the passport-only print view. Return here to share what was useful and what was confusing.

About ten minutes. No account or purchase required. You can use sample race values; please do not share personal health information or sensitive race notes.

Privacy: this page does not submit or store your answers on a TriZentro server. Download creates a local text file. The email option opens your own mail app with a draft; you decide whether to send it to trizentro.team@gmail.com. If you send it, your email provider and ours process the message, including your email address. Avoid sensitive health details and unnecessary personal information.
