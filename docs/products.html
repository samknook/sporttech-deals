Questions before you try TriZentro

Are the Race Pack and Blueprint available to buy?

Not yet. Our prototype and sample chapters are free. The displayed €19.95 prices are product hypotheses, not active offers. There is no checkout or delivery promise.

Why would I pay if the calculator is free?

You should only pay if the completed edition solves an additional problem for you. The proposed Blueprint will add professionally reviewed examples, printable exercises and expanded guidance. A future Race Pack would add a substantially more polished, reusable and detailed race dossier. If those additions are not valuable, keep using the free tools.

Do I need to make an account or share my race details?

No. Our free planning tools run in your browser. Feedback is also voluntary: you can download it or open an email draft to share it yourself. Check our privacy and transparency page for details.

Can I use TriZentro instead of a qualified coach or race briefing?

No. The tools model user-supplied assumptions, not individual physical fitness, medical readiness or actual course conditions. Always follow event officials and seek qualified individual advice when needed.
