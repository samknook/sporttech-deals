# TriZentro business operating plan
Updated 5 November 2026. Stage: product validation. The brand is **not yet a registered BV**.

## Customer
First-time and intermediate 70.3 triathletes who need a clear plan spanning pacing, transitions, preparation and race morning.

## Product ladder
- Free: Readiness Check, Race Dashboard, race-week guide, Race Pack preview.
- Free Race Pack already includes scenarios, checkpoints, timeline, checklist, notes, text export, print and optional local draft saving.
- Proposed premium: a substantially more useful, carefully edited and reviewed race dossier, reusable planning templates and polished accessible exports. **Not yet developed or for sale.**
- Proposed Blueprint: original chapters and exercises, still an unfinished manuscript.

## User journey
Organic discovery -> readiness check -> race plan -> free Race Pack preview -> voluntary tester feedback -> validated commercial offer.
No analytics, verified traffic, sales, or checkout. Do not report invented conversions.

## Ten-person pilot (opt-in only)
Invite four first-time 70.3 prospects, three athletes moving up from Sprint/Olympic and three with 70.3 experience. Ask them to test the mobile navigation, enter a 05:30 target using digits 530, save/reload the local Race Pack draft, export a plan, and describe missing value.

Decision thresholds (aspirations, not results): at least 8/10 complete the core flow without help, at least five provide actionable feedback, and at least five can name meaningful added paid value. Fix critical usability, calculation or safety issues before considering sales.

See TESTER_LAUNCH_KIT.md for outreach text. Never use unsolicited mass outreach.

## Development order
P0 public deployment and green Node/browser tests.
P1 reliable mobile and bilingual planning, local draft save/load/delete and clear instructions.
P2 ten genuine testers and anonymized issue ranking.
P3 differentiated premium deliverables and subject-matter review.
P4 legal operator, tax, consumer rights, payment, invoices, fulfillment and customer support before checkout.

## BV formation decision
TriZentro is a project name, not an incorporated entity. No trade registration or KVK number should be invented.
The Dutch KVK explains that incorporating a BV requires a notary, KVK and UBO registration, and entails setup and ongoing obligations; 2026 guidance indicates notary costs around €500–€1,500. A sole proprietorship may be an alternative depending on actual revenue and circumstances. A Dutch accountant/notary should evaluate structure, liability and taxes before any paid setup.

Sources:
- https://www.kvk.nl/starten/de-besloten-vennootschap-bv/
- https://www.kvk.nl/inschrijven/inschrijven-besloten-vennootschap-of-naamloze-vennootschap/

No notary, financial commitment, domain purchase, commerce account or business registration without the owner's authorization.
