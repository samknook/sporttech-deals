HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to a smarter race plan.

01 · Choose a distance

Sprint (750 m / 20 km / 5 km), Olympic (1.5 / 40 / 10 km), 70.3 (1.9 / 90 / 21.1 km), Full (3.8 / 180 / 42.2 km) or a custom course.

02 · Enter your pace

Use a swim pace per 100 m, average bike speed in km/h, run pace per km and both transitions.

03 · Review the scenario

See your projected finish time, individual splits and race-day checkpoints. Test a target time if you have one.

TriZentro provides arithmetic scenarios, not training advice or guarantees. Official course measurements and race rules always take precedence.
