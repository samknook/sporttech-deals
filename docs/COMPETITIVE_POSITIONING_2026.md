# TriZentro — competitive positioning
Research date: 5 November 2026
Stage: exploratory desk research, not a verified pricing survey or trademark clearance.

## Market reality
Several competitors already give substantial functionality away. Do not assume that a free calculator and checklist can be wrapped in a €19.95 PDF and sold profitably.

| Provider | Observed public offering | Strategic implication | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| FORM Endurance Race Planner | Browser-local race workspace, event logistics, route/course lab, guide and backup tools | Storage and full planning are not premium differentiation on their own | https://formendurance.com/race-planner/ |
| TriathlonPace | Free configurable race-day packing checklist | Basic packing lists are commodity | https://triathlonpace.com/tools/race-checklist-generator |
| Total Athlete | A/B/C race goal predictor, pacing and downloadable PDF | Three scenarios and PDF alone are not unique | https://total-athlete.nl/race-predictor/ |
| Triquatica | Event-specific race-morning tool incorporating published schedules | Official event-specific information can be valuable, but requires careful sourcing and upkeep | https://triquatica.online/en/events/ironman-703-malaga-2026/tools/morning-plan/ |
| Triathlon Companion app | Free features advertised include race checklists, GPX and forecasts | Competitive pressure from free mobile products; verify current functionality directly before making claims | https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.angevare.triathlon_companion |

## Strategic decision
Do **not** launch the proposed €19.95 Race Pack solely on the present scenario calculator, eight-point checklist or ordinary text export. These are free elsewhere too.

TriZentro's differentiation hypothesis is an approachable, bilingual, start-to-finish first-70.3 execution experience:
1. Help athletes interpret the *official* athlete guide rather than fabricate event dates or rules.
2. Turn official constraints (transition closing time, wave start, gear check-in requirements) and athlete-owned assumptions into one plan.
3. Show conflicts between personal schedule and the *user-entered* organizer deadlines.
4. Explain limitations and provenance so users distinguish official event facts from their own estimates.
5. Offer a clean, editable, re-usable and carefully reviewed printable plan that works without an account.
6. Include actual user-tested examples and checklists that make the next action obvious.

Potential premium extensions should be tested for actual willingness to pay; some may be best kept free and monetized via authorized partnerships or other value-added services.

## Follow-up research
- Ask first-time 70.3 athletes which decision caused the most confusion.
- Compare speed to first useful plan against competitors on a real phone.
- Survey whether event-specific official information, clear contingency instructions, or polished reusable dossiers are worth paying for.
- Run a brand/trade-name and domain rights check before purchase or legal registration. Search alone does not establish that TriZentro is available as a protected mark.
- Never state competitors' live pricing or features as permanent without re-checking dates.

## Current go/no-go
No paying customers, conversion data or tester feedback have been documented. Commercial launch stays on hold. The free Race Pack should be polished and genuinely useful as a validation tool; premium scope is not settled.
